Faith Daily Bible

Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026 · Hardaway Labs LLC

Faith Daily Bible collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and nothing to buy. What you save in the app stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your highlights, bookmarks, notes, reading plan progress, chosen translation and text size are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

The one network request

The New International Version, New Living Translation and New King James Version are licensed and load over the internet. When you open a chapter in one of them, the app asks our server for that chapter (for example, "NLT, John 3"). The request contains the translation and chapter only, with no identifier for you or your device. Our server fetches the text from API.Bible (American Bible Society) and returns it; the chapter is kept in the app's cache for the period the publisher allows.

Like any web server, ours and our hosting provider (Supabase) may briefly keep standard request logs, such as IP address and time, to keep the service running and secure. We don't use them to identify or track you.

The Berean Standard Bible, King James Version and Reina-Valera 1909 are built into the app and work with no connection at all.

Links to other apps

"Study" and "Healthy Faith" buttons open our separate app, Healthy Faith, or its store page. Only the Bible reference (book, chapter, verse) is passed. Healthy Faith has its own privacy policy. Sharing a verse uses your phone's share sheet; what you share and where is up to you.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Questions: support@healthyoneapp.com.
