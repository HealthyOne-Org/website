Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026 · Hardaway Labs LLC

Reading needs no account, and nothing we receive identifies you. The app sends anonymous usage counts (that it was opened, which translation a chapter was read in, that audio was played) under a random ID that isn't linked to your name, email or account. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy. Circles are the one place we keep personal information: to read a plan with a group and talk about it, you sign in, and then we keep your account and what you post in your circles. No advertising, nothing to buy, and nothing is sold or used to track you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your phone

Your highlights, bookmarks, notes, reading challenges and their progress, memory verses, chosen translation and text size are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Anonymous usage counts

To see what people use, the app sends a few counts to our analytics provider (PostHog):

  • the app was opened,
  • a chapter was read, and in which translation (not which book or chapter),
  • chapter audio was played, and in which translation,
  • the welcome screens were finished.

Each count carries a random ID created on your phone, the app version, and whether it's an iPhone or Android phone. It never includes your name, email, account, what you read, your highlights or notes, or anything you type, and it is never linked to your account even if you sign in. There are no screen recordings, no advertising IDs, and no profile is built about you. The counts are not used for advertising and are not sold or shared with anyone else.

To stop sending them, turn off Settings › Privacy › Share anonymous usage counts. Deleting the app also deletes the random ID.

If you sign in (circles only)

Circles are small groups reading the same plan. A circle needs to know who is speaking, so joining one asks you to sign in with Apple or with an email address. When you do, we keep:

  • Your account: an account ID, your email address (with Sign in with Apple this can be Apple's private relay address), and the display name you choose.
  • Your circles: which circles you belong to, the messages, prayer requests, reactions and quiz scores you post in them, and reports or blocks you make.

Only members of a circle can see what is posted in it, and only people who share a circle with you can see your display name. Before a message is posted it is checked automatically for abuse and signs of distress by a moderation service (OpenAI's moderation API), which receives the message text only. Messages that fail the check are not posted.

Your account works in our other app, Healthy Faith, as well, because both apps use the same sign-in.

Deleting your account: Settings › Account › Delete Account removes your account, your profile, your circle memberships and your messages. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Network requests

Licensed translations (New International Version, New Living Translation, New King James Version, NASB, Amplified, NIrV and NVI) load over the internet. When you open a chapter in one of them, the app asks our server for that chapter (for example, "NLT, John 3"). The request contains the translation and chapter only, with no identifier for you or your device. The Berean Standard Bible, King James Version and Reina-Valera 1909 are built into the app and work with no connection at all.

Sharing a reading challenge as a link stores that plan (its title and daily readings, nothing about you) on our server so the link can open it.

Like any web server, ours and our hosting provider (Supabase) may briefly keep standard request logs, such as IP address and time, to keep the service running and secure. We don't use them to identify or track you.

Links to other apps

"Ask" and "Healthy Faith" buttons open our separate app, Healthy Faith, or its store page. Only the Bible reference (book, chapter, verse) is passed. Healthy Faith has its own privacy policy. Sharing a verse uses your phone's share sheet; what you share and where is up to you.

Children

Reading works for everyone without any personal information. Circles are meant for people 13 and older; we don't knowingly keep accounts for younger children, and will delete one if we learn of it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Questions or deletion requests: support@healthyoneapp.com.
