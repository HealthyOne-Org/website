Seven apps, iPhone and Android
HealthyOne
AI calorie & nutrient tracker · our flagship

Snap a photo. Get the full nutrition.

Log a meal with a photo, a barcode, your voice, or a few words. HealthyOne reads calories, protein and 50+ nutrients, and it is built for people eating less on a GLP-1, when protein matters more than ever.

  • Photo, barcode, voice or text logging in seconds
  • 50+ nutrients, not just calories, with gaps flagged
  • GLP-1 mode raises your protein target to protect muscle
  • Syncs with Apple Health

Healthy Faith
Bible study & sermon notes · iPhone and Android

Record the sermon. Keep the notes.

Hit record in the pew, or paste a church livestream link, and Healthy Faith turns the sermon into study notes you can return to all week: main points, every verse linked, takeaways.

  • Sermon notes from a recording or a YouTube link
  • A calm Bible reader, or press Listen and let it read to you
  • Strong's word study on any verse
  • A daily Today page, reading challenges, and circles with friends

Faith Daily Bible
The whole Bible, free · from the Healthy Faith team

A Bible worth opening every day.

A clean, distraction-free Bible in NIV, NLT, NKJV and more, with reading plans that fit a real month. No account needed to start reading.

  • Full Bible, free, in modern translations
  • Plans like "John in 21 Days" and "30 Psalms for Hard Days"
  • A Today page that opens straight to your next reading
  • Hands off to Healthy Faith when you want sermon notes and word study

Budget Shield
Safe-to-spend & bill guard · iPhone

One number before payday. No more bounced bills.

Budget Shield shows you what you can spend right now without bouncing a bill before your next paycheck, and it texts you days before a payment would come up short.

  • A single safe-to-spend number, updated as you go
  • Bill guard warns you before a bill would bounce
  • Budgets by category, so every dollar has a job
  • A 12-month forecast of your low point
Download on theApp Store Learn more →
Recipella
Recipe saver & grocery list · iPhone and Android

Every recipe you love, in one cozy place.

Saw a dish on TikTok or Instagram? Paste the link and Recipella turns it into a clean, cook-ready recipe card with a grocery list sorted by aisle.

  • Paste a TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or website link
  • Grandma-style recipe cards you can actually cook from
  • Grocery list sorted by aisle, plus an estimated meal cost
  • Free for your first 20 recipes

Healthy Anger Management
Pause-and-respond coaching · iPhone

Respond, don't react.

For adults whose worst moments come from reacting before thinking. Tap when tension rises and Healthy Anger Management walks you through the pause: breathe, reframe, respond.

  • One big "I'm Triggered" button when it counts
  • Breathwork that resets your nervous system in under 90 seconds
  • Personality-aware coaching, not generic advice
  • Partner sync for the people who live with you

Healthy Bump
Pregnancy nutrition · iPhone

Growing a baby changes what your body needs.

Type what you ate and Healthy Bump identifies every food and tracks the nutrients that matter most in pregnancy, so you know your baby is getting what they need this week.

  • 15 pregnancy-critical nutrients, scored every day
  • Your prenatal vitamin counts toward the day
  • Week-by-week baby size, kick counter, trimester tips
  • Logging by text, photo, or label scan

Christopher Hardaway

Founder, Hardaway Labs LLC · Georgia

After years of trying every health and nutrition app on the market and finding them all tedious, Christopher set out to build something better: tools that use AI to remove the friction, respect your privacy, and meet you where you are.

Today that is seven apps, built and supported by a small team in Georgia, each one shaped by the same question: what would make this easier for a real person on a real Tuesday?
