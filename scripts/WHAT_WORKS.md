## Patterns

- Highly specific food lists for common situations, especially those involving reduced appetite or specific dietary needs, consistently attract high traffic and affiliate taps.
- Posts that directly answer a user's need for a tool or solution (even if not 'best app' comparisons) drive strong app taps.
- Titles that precisely match high-intent search phrases, often including a specific situation or a popular term, are highly effective.
- Content offering practical, actionable advice with clear food examples or portion guidance resonates well with readers.
