1 What the app is

Healthy Faith is a Bible study app: a reader with Strong’s word study, guided paths of scripture for hard seasons, a private journal, reading challenges, narrated scripture, and AI-assisted sermon notes.

2 Subscriptions and payment

Reading the Bible, word study, guided paths, and the journal are free. A Healthy Faith subscription unlocks the paid features described in the app at the time you subscribe.

Subscriptions are billed through Apple or Google, not by us. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

The King James Version is in the public domain. Other included translations are used under the terms their publishers grant. Scripture text is provided for personal study. You may quote and share passages as those publishers permit; you may not extract or redistribute the bundled text as a dataset or as a competing product.

4 AI-generated content

Answers, sermon notes, and reading plans are generated by AI. They are grounded in scripture by design, but AI can be wrong. Treat everything it produces as a study aid to be checked against the text, not as authoritative teaching, doctrine, or counsel. Test it against scripture and against your church.

Narrated audio is machine-generated speech, not a human reading.
