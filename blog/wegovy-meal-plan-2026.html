Wegovy Meal Plan: A Simple 7-Day Guide for Semaglutide (2026)

Wegovy solves the biggest problem in weight loss: it quiets the food noise and shrinks your appetite. But it creates a new, quieter problem. Your appetite is gone, but your body's need for protein to protect muscle during weight loss is higher than ever. You are asked to fit more protein through a smaller door.

A standard diet plan doesn't work. The portions are too big, the fat content is too high for a slow-moving stomach, and the protein is an afterthought. You need a plan built for how you eat now: small portions, protein first, and gentle on the system.

This is that plan. Seven days of simple meals designed for a semaglutide appetite, with protein targets for every meal and specific swaps for the days you feel nauseous.

The Core Principles of a Wegovy Meal Plan

Forget everything you know about 'dieting.' Your goals on this medication are different. Success isn't about restriction; it's about prioritization.

  • Protein First, Always. Every meal starts with protein. Aim for 25-35 grams per meal. If you only manage a few bites, make them the chicken, fish, or eggs, not the rice or bread.
  • Small, Frequent Meals. Four to five small meals or snacks are better than three large ones. This prevents feeling overly full and helps you meet your protein goal.
  • Low Fat, High Fiber. Fat is the primary trigger for nausea because it slows digestion further. Choose lean proteins. Fiber from non-starchy vegetables, like broccoli and spinach, helps with the constipation that often accompanies GLP-1s.
  • Hydrate Intelligently. Dehydration makes side effects worse. Sip water all day. Avoid chugging a large glass with a meal, which can make you feel too full. Add electrolyte powders if needed.

Your target is around 100 grams of protein per day. That's not a suggestion; it's the minimum required to signal to your body to burn fat, not muscle. This plan is designed to get you there.

A 7-Day Sample Wegovy Meal Plan

This is a template. Portions are small. Listen to your body and stop when you feel satisfied, not stuffed. The protein numbers are estimates to guide you.

Day 1: Gentle Start

  • Breakfast (25g protein): 1 cup nonfat Greek yogurt with 1 tablespoon of chia seeds.
  • Lunch (28g protein): 3 oz canned tuna (in water), mixed with 1 tablespoon of Greek yogurt, served with cucumber slices.
  • Snack (13g protein): 1 hard-boiled egg and 1 stick of string cheese.
  • Dinner (30g protein): 3 oz baked cod with steamed green beans.
  • Daily Total: ~96g protein

Day 2: Building Consistency

  • Breakfast (30g protein): Protein shake (1 scoop whey isolate blended with water or skim milk).
  • Lunch (30g protein): 3 oz shredded rotisserie chicken with a half-cup of quinoa.
  • Snack (14g protein): 1/2 cup 2% cottage cheese.
  • Dinner (32g protein): 4 oz lean ground turkey (93/7) scrambled with bell peppers and onions.
  • Daily Total: ~106g protein

Day 6 & 7: Weekend Flexibility

Repeat your favorite days or mix and match. Maybe you want an omelet for breakfast or a simple chicken salad for lunch. The key is to keep the principles the same: protein-dense, low-fat, and small portions.

Easy-on-the-Stomach Variations (For Titration Weeks)

When you increase your dose, nausea and food aversion can spike. During these days, your meal plan needs to get even simpler and gentler. Use these swaps:

  • Instead of grilled chicken or turkey, choose poached or shredded chicken.
  • Instead of lean beef, stick to white fish like cod or tilapia.
  • Instead of whole eggs, use scrambled egg whites. The yolk contains fat that can be hard to digest.
  • Instead of salmon, choose shrimp or scallops.
  • Rely more on cold, soft proteins: nonfat Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, and tofu.
  • Make your protein shake with water instead of milk and sip it over an hour.

What to Do on Low-Appetite Days

Some days, solid food is impossible. Do not just skip eating. That's a direct path to muscle loss and fatigue. On these days, your goal is liquid and soft nutrition.

A 'bare minimum' day might look like this:

  • Morning: A ready-to-drink protein shake (25-40g protein) sipped over 1-2 hours.
  • Mid-day: A cup of nonfat Greek yogurt (15-20g protein).
  • Afternoon: A cup of bone broth (10g protein) and an electrolyte drink.
  • Evening: A half-cup of cottage cheese (14g protein).

This provides 60-80 grams of protein with almost no chewing and minimal volume. It's not ideal, but it's far better than zero. It's a bridge to get you to the next day when your appetite returns.

FAQ

What should I eat for breakfast on Wegovy?

Focus on protein. A cup of nonfat Greek yogurt, a scramble of two eggs with one extra egg white, or a simple protein shake are all good options. They provide 20-30 grams of protein without the volume or fat that can cause morning nausea.

How many meals should I eat a day on semaglutide?

Most people do better with four to five small meals or snacks rather than three large ones. This approach helps manage appetite, keeps energy stable, and makes it easier to reach your daily protein goal without feeling overly full at any one sitting.

Can I drink alcohol on Wegovy?

Alcohol can worsen side effects like nausea and has a lot of empty calories. It also lowers your inhibitions around food choices. If you do drink, choose a single light beer or a clear spirit with soda water, and have it with a protein-heavy meal.

What snacks are good on a Wegovy meal plan?

Good snacks are protein-centric. A hard-boiled egg, a stick of string cheese, a handful of edamame, or a small cup of cottage cheese work well. They provide 6-14 grams of protein to help you bridge the gap between smaller meals.

The Bottom Line

A successful Wegovy meal plan isn't about deprivation; it's about strategy. Prioritize protein at every meal, keep portions small, and choose foods that are gentle on a slow-moving digestive system.
