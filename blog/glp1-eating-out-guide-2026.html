Eating Out on a GLP-1: What to Order at 12 Chains Without Regretting It (2026)

October 19, 2026 · 8 min read · By Chris Hardaway
Photo via Pexels

Nobody warns you that restaurants become a problem on a GLP-1. Not because of willpower, which the medication has mostly taken off the table, but because every menu is built for a stomach that empties at normal speed and an appetite that can handle 900 calories in a sitting. Yours now holds four or five bites, and if those bites are the wrong ones you spend the evening nauseous and the next morning wondering why you bothered.

The good news is that ordering well on Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro or Zepbound is a short list of rules plus a little knowledge of what the common chains actually serve. Here is both: the four rules first, then the order that works at 12 places you will end up at anyway.

The Four Rules for Any Menu

  • Protein first, then stop. Order the grilled chicken, the fish, the steak tips, the eggs. Eat that part of the plate first. Whatever comes after is optional and most nights you will not get to it.
  • Grilled over fried, always. Fried coating plus oil is the single most reliable nausea trigger on a GLP-1. A slow stomach holds that for hours.
  • Skip the bread basket and the drink refills. Bread is volume with no protein. Carbonation expands in a stomach that is not moving and brings reflux with it.
  • Ask for the box when you order. Half the plate goes in before you start. It stops you pushing past full, which on a GLP-1 is the line between fine and sick.

Eat slowly. Restaurant pace is set by the table, not your stomach. Put the fork down between bites and give the fullness signal time to arrive, because on these medications it shows up late and all at once.

What to Order at 12 Chains

Chipotle

Order: a bowl with double chicken or steak, fajita veggies, salsa, lettuce, no rice or half rice. About 50 g of protein; eat the meat first and box the rest. Skip: the burrito, queso, chips, and sour cream.

Chick-fil-A

Order: the grilled chicken sandwich without the bun, or grilled nuggets (8 count is 25 g), with a side salad or fruit cup. Skip: anything fried, the waffle fries, and the milkshakes.

Panera

Order: the Greek salad with chicken or a half Mediterranean bowl with chicken. The soups are mostly carbs and cream. Skip: the bread bowl, the mac and cheese, the bakery case.

Starbucks

Order: egg white bites (two is 13 g), or a protein box with the eggs and cheese. A tall latte with skim milk adds 8 g you can sip. Skip: the frappuccinos, the pastries, and the sweet cream cold foam.

Subway

Order: a rotisserie chicken or turkey protein bowl, which is the sandwich filling without the bread, loaded with veggies. Skip: the footlong, the meatball marinara, and creamy sauces.

Olive Garden

Order: herb-grilled salmon or grilled chicken with broccoli instead of pasta. Eat the protein, take two breadsticks home for someone else. Skip: the alfredo, the lasagna, and the bottomless salad before the entree arrives.

Chili's or Applebee's

Order: grilled chicken or sirloin with steamed broccoli, or the grilled shrimp. Skip: the appetizer sampler, loaded fries, and ribs.

Panda Express

Order: grilled teriyaki chicken or string bean chicken with super greens instead of rice. Skip: orange chicken, anything "crispy," and the chow mein.

Wendy's or McDonald's

Order: a grilled chicken sandwich with the bun removed, or a plain hamburger patty with a side salad. Egg McMuffin without the muffin is a surprisingly reasonable breakfast. Skip: fries, nuggets, shakes, and the double anything.

Sushi restaurants

Order: sashimi, edamame, miso soup, and a seaweed salad. Six pieces of sashimi is about 20 g with no rice to fill you first. Skip: tempura rolls, anything with cream cheese or spicy mayo, and big rice-heavy rolls.

Mexican sit-down

Order: chicken or steak fajitas and eat the meat and peppers with one tortilla or none. Black beans on the side. Skip: the chip basket, chimichangas, and queso fundido.

Diners and breakfast places

Order: a veggie omelet or egg white scramble with a side of turkey sausage or Canadian bacon. Skip: pancakes, waffles, pork sausage, and hash browns, which are the fat and sugar stack that ruins a morning on semaglutide.

The Dishes That Cause the Most Trouble

Across every chain the same four categories come up when GLP-1 users talk about the meal they regretted:

  • Fried anything — wings, tenders, fish and chips, tempura. The oil sits for hours.
  • Cream-based sauces and soups — alfredo, queso, chowder, bisque. Fat plus volume.
  • Large bread and rice portions — they fill the small window before the protein arrives.
  • Sugary drinks and desserts — a crash on top of a stomach that has eaten almost nothing.

Why the Restaurant Meal Is Where Tracking Falls Apart

At home you know what went on the plate. At a restaurant you ate roughly half of something with an unknown amount of oil, and the menu's calorie line is for the whole thing. Most people either skip logging the meal entirely or log the full menu item, and both are wrong by a lot. On a GLP-1 that error matters, because the restaurant night is often the only day you got near your protein number, and you cannot tell.

FAQ

What is the best fast food to eat on Ozempic?

Grilled chicken without the bun is the safest order almost everywhere: Chick-fil-A grilled nuggets or sandwich, a Wendy's or McDonald's grilled chicken with the bun removed, or a Chipotle bowl with double chicken and no rice. Eat the protein first and skip fries, fried items and shakes.

Why do I feel sick after eating at a restaurant on Wegovy?

Restaurant food is higher in fat and larger in portion than home cooking, and Wegovy slows how fast your stomach empties. Fried food, cream sauces and big plates sit for hours and cause nausea and reflux. Order grilled, stop at half, and avoid carbonated drinks with the meal.

Can I drink alcohol with dinner on a GLP-1?

It hits harder and faster because you have eaten less, and it adds to the dehydration these medications already cause. If you drink, keep it to one, have it with the protein rather than before it, and expect it to feel like two.

Should I log a restaurant meal if I only ate half?

Yes, and log the half, not the menu item. The menu calories describe the full plate. A photo before and after, or a voice note like 'half the salmon and the broccoli,' gets you a real protein number for the day instead of a guess that is off by half.
