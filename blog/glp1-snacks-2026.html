GLP-1 Snacks: 25 Snacks That Add Protein Without the Nausea (2026)

October 19, 2026 · 7 min read · By Chris Hardaway
Photo via Pexels

Snacking means something different on a GLP-1. Before the medication, a snack was the thing between meals that got you into trouble. Now the meals themselves are three or four bites, and a snack is how you make up the protein you could not fit at lunch. If you are on Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro or Zepbound and skipping snacks because you "aren't hungry," you are probably finishing the day 30 or 40 grams of protein short without noticing.

The problem is that most snack foods are the wrong shape for this. Chips, crackers, granola bars and trail mix are built to be eaten in volume, and volume is exactly what you no longer have. What works instead is small, dense, protein-first and gentle on a stomach that empties at half speed. Here are 25 that fit, plus the ones to avoid on the weeks your dose goes up.

The One Rule for GLP-1 Snacks

A snack earns its place if it delivers at least 8 grams of protein in something you can finish in under five bites. That is the whole test. If it fails, it is not a snack on a GLP-1. It is a nausea risk with no upside.

Timing matters more than it used to. Eat a snack two to three hours after a meal, not right after. The stomach is still working on lunch for a long time on these medications, and stacking food on top of food is how reflux and queasiness start.

Tier 1: Protein-First Snacks That Sit Well

These are the ones to keep in the fridge and the bag. Cold, soft or dry, low in fat, and dense enough that a few bites count.

  • Nonfat Greek yogurt cup — 15 to 17 g. The single most reliable snack on a queasy day.
  • Cottage cheese, half cup — 12 to 14 g. Add a few berries if you want it to feel like food.
  • Two hard-boiled eggs — 12 g. Boil six on Sunday and the week is covered.
  • String cheese, two sticks — 12 to 14 g. The low-moisture mozzarella kind is lighter than cheddar.
  • Deli turkey, rolled up — 4 slices is about 12 g. No bread needed.
  • Beef or turkey jerky, one ounce — 9 to 11 g. Pick low sugar, chew slowly.
  • Small can of tuna or salmon — 20 to 25 g. The pouches need no can opener.
  • Half a protein shake — 12 to 15 g. Sip it, do not chug it. A full shake can feel like a brick.
  • Edamame, half cup shelled — 9 g plus fiber for the constipation side of things.
  • Shrimp cocktail, six pieces — 12 g, essentially no fat.
  • Egg bites, two — 10 to 13 g. The store-bought sous vide kind reheat in a minute.
  • Cold shredded chicken, a few forkfuls — 3 oz is 26 g. Leftovers are the best snack there is.

Tier 2: Fine in Small Amounts

Good snacks for a normal appetite that need a portion cap on a GLP-1, either because the fat is higher or because they fill you up before the protein arrives.

  • Almonds or pistachios, a small handful — 6 g and 160 calories. A spoonful, not a bag.
  • Peanut butter on apple slices — one tablespoon is 4 g. The apple takes up space; keep it to half.
  • Hummus with cucumber — a quarter cup is 5 g. Skip the pita.
  • Protein bar — only if it is 15 g or more of protein and under 5 g of sugar. Half a bar is often enough.
  • Roasted chickpeas, a quarter cup — 5 g and crunchy, but dry and filling. Drink water with them.
  • Cheese and a few whole-grain crackers — keep the crackers to three or four so the cheese does the work.
  • Skim milk latte — 8 to 10 g in a tall one. Warm liquids go down easily on a slow morning.
  • Protein pudding or protein yogurt drinks — 10 to 20 g. Read the sugar line first.

Snacks That Cause Trouble on a GLP-1

None of these are forbidden forever. All of them are the ones people regret on dose-increase weeks.

  • Chips, pretzels and popcorn — zero protein, pure volume, and the salt makes the dehydration worse.
  • Granola bars and trail mix — sugar plus fat plus bulk is the worst possible combination for a slow stomach.
  • Fried snacks of any kind — mozzarella sticks, chicken nuggets, egg rolls. The oil sits for hours.
  • Ice cream and milkshakes — the fat and sugar together are the most common nausea complaint after fried food.
  • Carbonated drinks, even diet — the gas expands in a stomach that is not moving, and reflux follows.
  • Big smoothies — a 24-ounce fruit smoothie is 400 calories of sugar your appetite cannot handle. If you want one, make it 10 ounces and put the protein in.
  • Candy and pastries — the sugar crash is sharper when you have eaten almost nothing else.

A Sample Snack Day That Reaches the Protein Floor

Say your three meals only managed 60 grams between them, which is common on a GLP-1. Two snacks close the gap without a single large portion.

  • Mid-morning: a Greek yogurt cup — 17 g.
  • Mid-afternoon: two string cheese sticks and a hard-boiled egg — 19 g.

That is 96 grams for the day, and the snacks took four minutes combined. The point was never to eat more. It was to make the small amount count.

Why Snacks Are Where GLP-1 Users Lose Track

Meals get logged because they feel like events. Snacks happen standing at the counter and vanish from memory. On a normal diet that is a rounding error. On a GLP-1, where snacks might be a third of your protein, forgetting them means you have no idea whether today was a 60-gram day or a 100-gram day, and the two have very different effects on your muscle over a year.

FAQ

Should I snack on Ozempic if I'm not hungry?

Usually yes, if your meals are small. On a GLP-1 the risk is not eating too much, it is finishing the day short on protein. A small protein snack two to three hours after a meal helps you reach 90 to 100 grams without a large plate. Skip it only if you are actively nauseous.

What are the best snacks for nausea on a GLP-1?

Cold, soft, low-fat and small: nonfat Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, a hard-boiled egg, plain deli turkey, or half a protein shake sipped slowly. Avoid fried, greasy, sugary or carbonated snacks, which slow an already slow stomach.

How many grams of protein should a GLP-1 snack have?

Aim for at least 8 grams per snack, and more is better. Two snacks of 10 to 17 grams each can close the gap between what your small meals provide and the 90 to 100 grams a day that protects muscle during rapid weight loss.

Are protein bars okay on Mounjaro or Zepbound?

Some are. Look for 15 grams of protein or more and under 5 grams of sugar, and start with half a bar since they are dense. Many bars are closer to candy and bring the sugar crash and bloating that make a slow stomach feel worse.

The Bottom Line

A snack on a GLP-1 is a small, protein-dense bridge between meals you could not finish. Keep yogurt, eggs, cheese, jerky and tuna within reach, eat them two to three hours after a meal, skip the fried and fizzy and sugary options on the weeks your dose goes up, and count them, because snacks are where most people on these medications quietly lose 30 grams of protein a day.
