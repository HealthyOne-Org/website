Zepbound Meal Plan: 7 Days of Small, Protein-First Meals (2026)

October 30, 2026 · 8 min read · By Chris Hardaway
Photo via Pexels

Zepbound changes what a meal is. Before tirzepatide, dinner was a plate. Now it might be four bites of chicken and a forkful of rice before your stomach says stop. That is the medication working, but it creates a new problem: most meal plans online were written for people with a normal appetite, and they hand you portions you will never finish.

This plan is built the other way around. Every meal is small, protein comes first, and each day lands between roughly 85 and 100 grams of protein, close to the range many GLP-1 users aim for to hold on to muscle while the scale drops. Your own number depends on your size and goals, so check it with your prescriber or a dietitian. If you have not read it yet, start with the Zepbound food list — this plan uses only foods from it.

How This Zepbound Meal Plan Works

Four rules make it work on a stomach that empties slowly. Mounjaro uses the same drug, tirzepatide, so the plan works the same way there, and it fits Ozempic and Wegovy users too.

  1. Protein first, every time. Eat the chicken, eggs or yogurt before anything else on the plate. If you stop after four bites, those four bites counted.
  2. Four or five small eating times, not three big ones. Breakfast, lunch, dinner, plus one or two small protein stops in between.
  3. Stop at the first sign of full. On Zepbound, the gap between "satisfied" and "sick" is a bite or two. Leftovers are fine.
  4. Drink between meals, not with them. Water with food fills the stomach and pushes protein off the plate. Sip through the day instead.

Portions here are a starting point. If a 3-ounce piece of chicken is too much this week, eat two ounces and add a yogurt cup later. The daily protein total matters more than finishing any single meal.

Sunday Prep: 20 Minutes

The plan only holds up if the protein is already cooked when you open the fridge. On Sunday:

  • Boil 6 eggs.
  • Bake or air-fry 1 pound of chicken breast and shred half of it.
  • Make a double batch of chicken salad with Greek yogurt instead of mayo.
  • Stock single-serve Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, string cheese and a few ready-to-drink protein shakes.

The 7-Day Zepbound Meal Plan

Grams are approximate and vary by brand. Totals are rounded. Swap anything using the list further down.

Day 1 — about 92 g protein

  • Breakfast: 3/4 cup nonfat Greek yogurt, a few blueberries — 17 g. Cold and soft, the easiest first meal of the week.
  • Lunch: tuna pouch mixed with a spoon of Greek yogurt, on cucumber rounds — 19 g. No bread, no mayo, nothing heavy.
  • Snack: 2 string cheese sticks — 14 g. Two to three hours after lunch, not right after.
  • Dinner: 3 oz chicken breast, 1/2 cup roasted carrots, 1/4 cup rice — 27 g. Eat the chicken first.
  • Evening: half a ready-to-drink protein shake — 15 g. Sip it over 20 minutes.

Day 2 — about 88 g protein

  • Breakfast: 2 scrambled eggs plus 1/4 cup egg whites, spinach — 19 g. Cook in a light spray, not butter.
  • Snack: 1/2 cup cottage cheese with pineapple — 13 g. Small bowl, slow spoon.
  • Lunch: 1 cup lentil soup with 2 oz shredded chicken stirred in — 26 g. Soup goes down on days solid food doesn't.
  • Dinner: 3 oz baked salmon, 1/2 cup green beans, half a small potato — 22 g. The salmon is the meal. The potato is optional.
  • Evening: 1 cup milk or kefir — 8 g. Fills a gap without feeling like food.

Day 3 — about 100 g protein

  • Breakfast: 1 ready-to-drink protein shake, sipped over 30 minutes — 30 g. For mornings when nothing solid sounds good.
  • Lunch: 3 oz deli turkey and 1 oz Swiss rolled up, a few crackers — 23 g. No cooking, packs in a bag.
  • Snack: 2 hard-boiled eggs — 12 g. Boiled on Sunday.
  • Dinner: 3 oz shrimp sautéed with zucchini, 1/4 cup orzo — 22 g. Shrimp is lean and cooks in four minutes.
  • Evening: 1/2 cup cottage cheese — 13 g. Plain, or with cinnamon.

Day 4 — about 98 g protein

  • Breakfast: 3/4 cup cottage cheese with sliced peach — 19 g. Canned peaches in juice are fine.
  • Lunch: chicken salad (3 oz chicken, Greek yogurt dressing) in lettuce cups — 28 g. Make a double batch for Day 5.
  • Snack: 1 oz low-sugar beef jerky — 10 g. Chew slowly.
  • Dinner: taco bowl — 3 oz lean ground turkey, 1/4 cup black beans, salsa — 26 g. Skip the cheese and sour cream pile.
  • Evening: half a protein shake — 15 g. Only if dinner came up short.

Day 5 — about 88 g protein

  • Breakfast: 2 egg-white bites and 1 string cheese — 19 g. Store-bought egg bites reheat in 60 seconds.
  • Lunch: 1 cup lean beef and bean chili — 20 g. Leftover-friendly and warm.
  • Snack: single-serve Greek yogurt cup — 15 g. Keep a few in the work fridge.
  • Dinner: 4 oz baked cod, 1/2 cup roasted squash, mashed cauliflower — 25 g. Mild, soft and easy on a slow stomach.
  • Evening: 1/2 cup shelled edamame — 9 g. Warm from the microwave.

Day 6 — about 98 g protein

  • Breakfast: 2-egg omelet with 1 oz ham and 1 oz cheese — 24 g. The weekend breakfast that still counts.
  • Lunch out: grilled chicken salad, dressing on the side — 24 g. Eat the chicken, leave the croutons.
  • Snack: 1/2 cup cottage cheese — 13 g. Before you go out for dinner, not after.
  • Dinner: 3 oz pork tenderloin, 1/2 cup roasted carrots — 22 g. Tenderloin is as lean as chicken.
  • Evening: half a protein shake — 15 g. Optional.

Day 7 — about 95 g protein

  • Breakfast: 1/3 cup oats cooked in milk, half a scoop of protein powder stirred in — 24 g. Warm, soft and high in fiber.
  • Lunch: 2 oz leftover pork with half a small baked potato — 17 g. Leftovers save Sunday.
  • Snack: 2 hard-boiled eggs — 12 g. Last two from the batch.
  • Dinner: chicken stir-fry — 3 oz chicken, peppers, 1/4 cup rice — 27 g. Light on the oil and soy sauce.
  • Evening: single-serve Greek yogurt cup — 15 g. Then start the next week's prep.

The Dose-Increase Week Version

The first week after a dose step-up is when nausea, fullness and food aversions tend to show up. Don't fight it with the regular plan. Switch to this lighter version for a few days and go back when your appetite settles:

  • Six mini meals instead of four or five — two or three bites every two to three hours.
  • Cold and bland protein — 15 to 17 g. Greek yogurt, cottage cheese and protein shakes sit better than hot, greasy food.
  • Broth with shredded chicken — 12 to 15 g. A cup of warm broth with 2 ounces of chicken for when solids feel like too much.
  • Crackers and string cheese — 8 to 10 g. Dry and plain, a classic on queasy days.
  • Scrambled egg whites — 10 to 13 g. Soft and low in fat.
  • Applesauce with a scoop of unflavored protein — 10 to 12 g. Easy to get down when nothing appeals.

Aim for 60 to 70 grams on the worst day instead of skipping protein entirely. If nausea, vomiting or not keeping fluids down lasts more than a day or two, call your prescriber. For more ideas, see what to eat when GLP-1 nausea hits.

Swap List: Trade Like for Like

Swap within the same line and the protein stays about the same:

  • Chicken breast (3 oz, ~26 g) ↔ turkey breast, pork tenderloin, shrimp, or 5 oz firm tofu.
  • Salmon (3 oz, ~22 g) ↔ cod, tilapia, canned salmon, or a can of tuna.
  • Greek yogurt (3/4 cup, ~17 g) ↔ skyr, 3/4 cup cottage cheese, or a half shake.
  • 2 eggs (~12 g) ↔ 1/2 cup egg whites, 2 string cheese, or 1 oz jerky.
  • Lentil soup (1 cup, ~10 g) ↔ black bean soup, split pea soup, or 1/2 cup edamame.
  • Rice or potato (small scoop) ↔ quinoa, orzo, a small corn tortilla, or skip it on low-appetite days.

Vegetarian? Use tofu, tempeh, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, edamame and lentils, and lean on shakes a little more. Need more ideas? The GLP-1 protein foods list ranks dozens of options by grams per bite.

What to Skip on Zepbound

These are the foods most likely to sit for hours or cause trouble on tirzepatide:

  • Fried food — fries, fried chicken, onion rings. Fat slows an already slow stomach even more.
  • Large, greasy meals — burgers, pizza, creamy pasta. One slice can feel like a full dinner and then some.
  • Sugary drinks and desserts — soda, sweet coffee drinks, pastries. Lots of calories, almost no protein.
  • Carbonated drinks — even diet soda and sparkling water can add pressure and bloating.
  • Alcohol — empty calories, and many people find they tolerate it worse on a GLP-1. Ask your prescriber what is safe for you.
  • Big salads as a whole meal — lots of volume, little protein. Put the chicken on top and eat it first.

Why a Printed Plan Isn't Enough

A meal plan assumes you eat what is written. On Zepbound you won't. You will finish half of Tuesday's dinner, skip Thursday's snack, eat out on Saturday and have no idea whether the day landed at 60 grams or 100. FAQ

How much protein should I eat on Zepbound?

Many GLP-1 users aim for roughly 90 to 100 grams a day to protect muscle while losing weight, which is what this plan targets. The right number depends on your body size, activity and health, so ask your prescriber or a dietitian to set yours.

Can I use this meal plan on Mounjaro?

Yes. Mounjaro and Zepbound are both tirzepatide, so appetite and fullness work the same way. The plan also fits Ozempic and Wegovy users, since all four slow digestion and shrink portions.

What should I eat the week my Zepbound dose goes up?

Go smaller and blander for a few days: six mini meals of Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, protein shakes, broth with shredded chicken, egg whites and crackers with string cheese. Skip fried and greasy food. If nausea or vomiting doesn't settle, call your prescriber.

Do I have to eat if I'm not hungry on Zepbound?

Usually you should still eat small amounts of protein through the day, even without hunger, so you don't fall far short on protein. Two or three bites of yogurt, eggs or chicken counts. If you can't eat at all, talk to your prescriber.

The Bottom Line

A Zepbound meal plan works when it is built for the appetite you actually have: small meals, protein first, four or five eating times a day and a lighter version for dose-increase weeks. Prep the protein on Sunday, swap freely within the list, skip the fried and fizzy, and track what you really ate, because on tirzepatide the plan and the plate rarely match.
