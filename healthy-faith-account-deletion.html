Healthy Faith — Account & Data Deletion

How to delete your Healthy Faith: Bible Study account and everything stored with it.

Last updated: July 2026

This page covers Healthy Faith: Bible Study, published by Hardaway Labs LLC on the App Store and Google Play. You own your data. You can request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time. We respond to every request and confirm by email when deletion is complete.

Reading the Bible does not require an account

Healthy Faith opens straight into the reader. The Bible text, chapter navigation, translations, and Strong’s word study all work with no sign in and no account. An account only exists if you created one to sync your journal and reading progress across devices.

1. Request deletion by email

Send us an email and we will delete your account and its data:

  1. Email support@healthyoneapp.com from the email address registered to your Healthy Faith account.
  2. Use the subject line: Healthy Faith Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your account in the body of the message.

We verify your identity using your registered email address, so the request must come from that address. If you no longer have access to it, say so in your message and we will work with you to verify another way.

Email a deletion request

What happens next

Your account is disabled as soon as we verify the request. All cloud-stored data listed below is permanently removed within 30 days. You will receive an email confirmation once deletion is complete.

2. What is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days:

Data type Deleted
Account profile (email address, sign-in credentials)Yes
Bible Challenge progress and reading plans you joinedYes
Journal entries synced to your account (Vision, Declarations, Revelations, Prayers)Yes
Sermon recordings you saved, their transcripts, and AI notesYes
Audio-reading usage records tied to your accountYes
Questions you asked and the answers returnedNot retained — never stored against your account
Sermon audio recordingsTranscribed on your device; the audio is not uploaded to our servers
Subscription billing recordsRetained as required by law (see below)
Aggregated, anonymized analyticsRetained — cannot be tied back to you

Data stored only on your device

Highlights, bookmarks, reading position, and the downloaded Bible text are stored only on your phone and are never uploaded to our servers. Journal entries are also written to your phone first; they are only copied to your account if you are signed in with Premium, and any copy we hold is deleted with your account. It is removed when you delete the app. We never receive a copy of it, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

3. Data we may retain

For legal, tax, and security reasons a small set of records survives account deletion:

  • Payment and subscription records — retained for the period required by US tax law (typically 7 years).
  • Security and abuse logs — retained for up to 12 months in aggregated form to protect against fraud and misuse.
  • Anonymized, aggregated analytics — cannot be linked to your identity once your account is deleted.

None of these records contain your journal entries, sermon notes, or reading history.

4. Cancelling your subscription

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active Healthy Faith Premium subscription — those are billed by Apple and Google, not by us. Cancel it separately:

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Healthy Faith → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play → tap your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Healthy Faith → Cancel.

Cancel before deleting your account, or you may continue to be billed for a subscription you can no longer use.

5. Questions or trouble

If you do not receive a confirmation, or have any other question about deletion, email support@healthyoneapp.com. We respond to deletion requests within 7 business days.
