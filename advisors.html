What they do

Our advisors approved the one-page guidelines below. Every week they get a short summary of our new articles with each health claim listed, and they reply OK or tell us what to fix. An article shows a review line only after that advisor has approved that exact article, for example:

Nutrition reviewed by [Name], RD · [date]

Articles without that line follow the same guidelines but have not been individually reviewed yet. Our advisors do not write our articles, sell products, or endorse the HealthyOne app.

Our content guidelines, in short

  • Natural first. We write about whole-food eating, protein and fiber, walking and strength training, sleep and steady habits.
  • Medication is your doctor’s call. We never tell anyone to start, stop or change a medication. Questions about a medicine or dose go to your prescriber.
  • No miracle claims. No cures, detoxes, “natural Ozempic” supplements or promised results.
  • No made-up science. No invented studies or statistics; only well-established basics.
  • Safety first. Start movement slowly, and check with a doctor or dietitian if you are pregnant, have a health condition or take medication.
  • Kind and practical. Plain words, real portions, no shame.
